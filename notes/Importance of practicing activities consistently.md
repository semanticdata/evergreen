It is difficult to choose where to begin because every option feels important. Naturally, uncertainty can become overwhelming: I am learning to write, but I may think that studying logic will strengthen my reasoning directly and give me more subjects to write about. Yet, while studying logic, I will still need to explain and reason through what I learn. I will still need to write those thoughts down clearly.

These are only examples. The important thing is to track learning progress.

The real challenge is not picking the right thing—it is staying with it. That is where bad habits creep in, and just writing is the only answer. Write reflections every day, especially every morning, and review them at the end of each week. It will be hard, but do it every day regardless of external circumstances. In the morning, run, meditate, and write. Give your words time to come. When you must choose between writing and hurrying to work, studying, or going on a trip, remember to [[devote yourself doing what you feel is truly important]]

Open a laptop and record the writing process in a Notion calendar. One hour is enough. Start by writing whatever is on your mind. Sometimes you may feel that you are forcing yourself to write; slow down and enjoy the process.
